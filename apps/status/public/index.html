About these measurements

Checks run every minute from one monitoring vantage point. Incidents require two failed checks; recovery requires three healthy checks. Slower successful checks count as available. Unknown time and scheduled maintenance are excluded from availability and shown separately. Web, Admin and bot depend on the API; these checks do not test human sign-in or every interaction.

The 24-hour window is rolling. The 30- and 90-day windows include today and the preceding UTC calendar days. History refreshes every 15 minutes; current state refreshes every 30 seconds. Each observation represents at most three minutes. These are sampled measurements, not an SLA.
